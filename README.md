# Clean Food Score

[CleanFoodScore.com](https://cleanfoodscore.com) checks packaged foods and restaurant menu items against your own additive avoid-list.

Pick the additives you want to avoid, then see which products pass, fail or lack verified ingredient evidence. Ingredient labels are copied verbatim from makers' stores and USDA FoodData Central.

## What you'll find

- Product ingredient checks against your personal avoid-list
- Shelf-by-shelf comparisons: tallow, butter, eggs, milk, protein powder, fiber, snacks and more
- Restaurant menu item checks
